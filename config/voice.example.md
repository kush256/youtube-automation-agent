# voice.md

Copy this file to `config/voice.md` (or set `VOICE_PROFILE_PATH`) and fill it in. The script
writer, the title picker and the description writer all read it before they write anything.
Ten minutes here is worth more than any prompt tweak: the narrator says these words out loud,
and a script in the wrong voice sounds wrong no matter how good the hook is.

Adapted from the voice template in the YouTube agent skill pack
(https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill, MIT).

## Who I am talking to

One person. Name them properly - not "creators", but "someone with under a thousand subs who has
made nine videos and cannot work out why none of them break 400 views".

## How I actually talk

Paste the transcript of your best-performing video here, or three of them. Not what you wish you
sounded like.

## Words I never use

The ones that are not mine. Be specific: "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it".

## Words I do use

Your tics. The phrases that are yours. Keep them.

## What I will not claim

Numbers I cannot show, results that are not mine, tools I have not used. Write them down so the
writers refuse them for you instead of you catching it in review.

## My format

Length, how a video opens, whether there is an intro, whether you swear, what the visuals look like.
